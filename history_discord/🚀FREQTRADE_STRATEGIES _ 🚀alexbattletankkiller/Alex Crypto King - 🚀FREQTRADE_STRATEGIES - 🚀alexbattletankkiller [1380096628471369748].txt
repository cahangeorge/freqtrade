[06/06/2025 13:56] anonymuis
TLDR: A strat like this cannot also handle all market exceptions.

I fully agree with you. But in reality, most automated trading systems are optimized for one function only. It is hard enough to create a strategy that performs good in an average market, it is almost impossible to make the strategy also handle all exceptions well.

If you have a strategy like this, reality is that you will have days with losses when market changes unexpectedly. If you code it well, the next day will cover those losses and you can continue to profit.

While we like the crypto market because of volatility, we cannot complain when that volatility goes against our plans! 😉

In the end, you need to balance risks (being 'in' the market when things take a turn) against chances of making a profit. Often making sure you don't take too much of a risk, also means making MUCH less profit.

Maybe we could think about adding a 'risk leverage' as a variable. If you set it to 10, you'll make as much profit as possible, but will also get fucked when market turns. If you set it to 1, you will hardly have days with losses, but profits will be very small.

I'm not a very good traider, but I can predict days that could influence the markets heavily. (Like just following Trump on X)
