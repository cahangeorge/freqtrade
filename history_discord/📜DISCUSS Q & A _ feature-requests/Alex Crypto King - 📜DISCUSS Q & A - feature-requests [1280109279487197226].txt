@Alex Crypto King For me the strategy attempts to execute a trade on every candle, either by entering a short or long position. However, when the maximum number of trades is set to 10 and there are more than 10 coins available, freqAI doesn't choose the trade with the highest confidence score. Instead, it simply trades on the next coin evaluated. Ideally, the bot should skip candles where the confidence level is in a uncertain zone, freeing up trade slots for coins where the bot has higher confidence in shorting or longing. While the thresholds for buy/sell signals should adjust this behavior, hyperopt doesn't seem to care about volume and will always favor results that gives more profit. This in my mind makes the threshold values are currently ineffective. Shouldn't as a trader you want a strategy to make fewer, but higher-confidence, trades?
