[09/10/2024 15:54] giovannimauro
Hello guys, here you can see the plot of the V9Hyper strategy, and as you can see, the red line, which represents the "&-target" indicator, is almost always flat (at zero), which means that when there's a peak or a valley, that's the moment to place a trade.
On the contrary, the Hyperopt results give a negative value for the buy_threshold and a positive one for the sell_threshold, which means, considering the entry conditions:
_df['&-target'] > self.threshold_buy.value for Longs
_df['&-target'] < self.threshold_sell.value for Shorts
That the signals are not present when &-target moves, but almost always, because &-target is almost always below that threshold.
I think this happens because the hyperopt looks for the best conditions to make a profit, and this aspect, combined with the fact that the strategy, through its "target_horizon=1", is absolutely Lookahead biased, creates a winning strategy in hyperopt and backtest, but in reality, it places continuous trades at each candle without criteria, as both entry conditions are almost always satisfied.
I may be on the wrong track, but that's what I noticed when running and plotting the Hyper strategies.
I think, therefore, that we should consider changing the logic, or forcing the threshold values to only one side.
