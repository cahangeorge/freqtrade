[01/16/2025 10:24] cs_0612_49562
Reason: Some pairs of candles are quite special, and in many cases, their high and low prices are equal. Their subtraction appears as a divisor in some features of tnkai, which causes many feature values to become Nan, leading to the error mentioned earlier.


[01/16/2025 10:25] cs_0612_49562
terms of settlement:
1. Add the following statement at the end of the   `feature_engineering_expand_basic` function (choose one of the two): `dataframe.filter(like='%%').fillna(method='ffill', inplace=True)` or `dataframe.filter(like='%%').fillna(0, inplace=True)`
2. If 1 does not work, set the `DI_threshold` in the config as large as possible, such as 100.
3. If 1 and 2 do not work, then increase the `train_period_days` in config or decrease the `window_size`。
4. If none of the above methods work, please put the problematic pair on the blacklist. Its data is really not suitable for LSTM training.

**Recommendations for Training a FreqAI Model:**
1. Avoid trying to predict price extremes directly, as the values will mostly be zero, leading to poor model training. Instead, predict the closing price and derive extremes from those predictions.  
2. Focus on predicting the actual price rather than percentage price changes.  
3. Exclude features with near-zero variance to reduce unnecessary noise.  
4. Adjusted the FreqAI config:  
   - Increased `train_period_days`  
   - Reduced `num_lstm_layers` and `hidden_dim`  
   - Set `dropout_percent` to 0.5  
   - and other small changes (output_dim etc)
