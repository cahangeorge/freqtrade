Alex_GKD_FisherTransform is a Fisher Transform momentum/trend strategy with ML-assisted filtering/optimization. It’s built to catch strong directional moves while reducing chop/noise through confirmations and confidence logic.

Key Benefits ✅

• Fisher Transform core: highlights momentum shifts and trend acceleration (good for catching “impulse” phases).
• Balanced LONG + SHORT logic: entries are designed to work symmetrically in both directions (no one-sided bias).
• ML confidence layer 🤖: uses a model/scaler pipeline to score conditions and help filter weaker setups (aim: fewer low-quality trades).
• Risk tightened vs earlier versions: stoploss and general risk controls were significantly tightened compared to older drafts (more realistic safety posture).
• Trade capacity capped: reduced max exposure by limiting max open trades (helps prevent overtrading during high volatility).
• Built for iteration: includes Optuna/ML model persistence paths so the system can improve via testing over time.

What it’s best for 🎯

• Markets with clear swings and momentum bursts
• Traders who value signal quality over constant entries
• Setups where shorts matter (not only long-only cycles)

Notes / Expectations ⚠️

This is still a powerful system (and can be complex due to ML). Start with DRY-RUN + backtests, keep leverage sane, and treat ML as a filter—not magic.
