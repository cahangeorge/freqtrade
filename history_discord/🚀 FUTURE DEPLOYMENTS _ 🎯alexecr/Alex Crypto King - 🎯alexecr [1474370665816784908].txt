ECRV2 — Strategy Overview (VIP) 🤘☠️

ECRV2 (Enhanced Crash‑Resistant V2) is a 5m futures strategy built around a crash‑resistant / recovery style approach. It combines indicator-based entries with risk controls + ATR-based exits, and includes Martingale DCA for LONGs (SHORT DCA is disabled by default for safety).

Key Benefits ✅

• LONG + SHORT enabled: V2 adds proper short entries/exits.
• ATR-based TP/SL (smart + symmetric): take‑profit and stop levels are derived from ATR at entry, working for both longs and shorts.
• Recovery / DCA engine (LONG only): can add up to 3 safety orders (max 4 entries total) to improve average entry during drawdowns.
• Hard exposure cap: prevents runaway DCA by limiting total exposure (max multiplier).
• Trailing stop enabled: helps lock profit when moves continue.
• Fast timeframe (5m): reacts quickly to volatility and mean reversion swings.

Entry Logic (high level) 🎯

• LONG: oversold + lower Bollinger area + MACD confirmation + volume check.
• SHORT: overbought + upper Bollinger area + MACD confirmation + volume check.

Risk / Reality Check ⚠️

ECRV2 is not a “set and forget” strategy — it’s a DCA/martingale-style system and must be used with care:

• use sensible leverage + stake sizing
• understand that DCA increases exposure during drawdowns
• always start with DRY‑RUN / backtests

Done! ✅ Just change this one line at the top of the strategy:

leverage_value = 10.0  # ← CONFIGURE LEVERAGE HERE

• 10.0 = 10x leverage
• 5.0 = 5x leverage
• 1.0 = 1x leverage (back to original)

The min() makes sure you don't request more than the exchange allows for that pair.
