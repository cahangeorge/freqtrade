[01/29/2025 14:12] ch1ck3n_t4c0s
The fft function is used to derive the optimal periods. As you decrease the timeframe you increase noise input to the fft so more Smoothing needs applied Ala bigger windows for the fft to see through the noise and find the correct periods. To low if a lower limit can inject more noise as well.  It's essentially a Bandpass filter.
