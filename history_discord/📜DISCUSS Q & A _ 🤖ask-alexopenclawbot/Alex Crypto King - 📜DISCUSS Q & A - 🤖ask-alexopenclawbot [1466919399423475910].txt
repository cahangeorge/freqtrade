**VisionLLM Strategy — Quick Summary** 🤘

**What it is:**
An AI-powered trading strategy where a local LLM (Qwen 2.5 via Ollama) makes entry and exit decisions by analyzing technical indicators — no hardcoded rules, the AI "thinks" about each trade.

**How it works:**
• **1h timeframe**, long & short enabled
• Every new candle, it feeds 20+ indicators (RSI, MACD, BB, ADX, Fisher, volume, S/R levels, etc.) to the LLM
• LLM decides: enter, hold, or pass — plus stop-loss, take-profit, position size, and leverage (1x/3x/5x)
