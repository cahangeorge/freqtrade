Yes mostly I have found that hyperopt, backtesting and live data should and be taken in as data. Due to functionality differences performed between live/dry and the HO/backtest the results can not always be correlated. Live ALWAYS has a rolling window with 0 forward information. Backtesting and hyperopt introduces this forward bias problem.  From another point of view using a prediction from regressor or what have you to predict the future and then save it and access at the appropriate time is not all that far off from forward bias. It's just a prediction instead of real forward data. The main problem is that freqtrade does not perform backtesting and hyperopts in a manner that matches live functionality.
Why? Because it's not performant and in most basic strategies not necessary as it doesn't drastically effect the outcomes. As you begin to step outside the 📦, it has to be considered.
