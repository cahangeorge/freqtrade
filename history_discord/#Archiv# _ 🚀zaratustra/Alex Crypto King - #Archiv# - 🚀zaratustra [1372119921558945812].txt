The idea is: 
— get data for top 500 coins (bybit, in my case)
— calculate correlation of coin prices against .... BTC (?)
— calculate volatility of each coin (ATR)
With these data, get most correlated & choose ATR volatility of your preference (most/least volatile). 

In this concept, many input params are yet to be tested and improved. For example,which of correlations to use (pearson? spearman? cosine? cointegration? anny other?)
Share your results please! 

*** a python script for alll that stuff is very easy to generate with any GPT*
