lexSignalForgeV4 (ASF4) — VIP Strategy Overview 🤘☠️

ASF4 is a non‑repainting futures signal strategy built for 15m execution with 1h market context. The goal: cleaner entries, fewer traps, smarter exits — designed for real market conditions, not just “nice charts”.

Key Benefits ✅

• Non‑repainting signals: decisions use closed candles only (confirmed data).
• 15m + 1h logic: trades on 15m, uses 1h trend as a soft filter (not a hard blocker) to stay flexible.
• Trap/false breakout filter: aims to avoid common pain points like wick-rejections and fake breakouts.
• Volatility spike protection: reduces entries during extreme volatility (often liquidation spikes / falling knives).
• Volume confirmation: prefers moves with real participation (volume ratio vs rolling mean), not weak drifts.

Entry Logic (high level) 🎯

• LONG: trend pullbacks + selective reversal setups when downside is rejected.
• SHORT: downtrend pullbacks + selective rejection at the top.
• Includes conflict handling so it won’t signal long & short at the same time.

Exit Logic (where edge is made) 🛡️

• Profit protection (peak tracking): locks in gains if price gives back too much after a strong run.
• Momentum exhaustion exits (RSI-based): takes profit when moves look stretched.
• Emergency protection: exits earlier if a trade gets dangerously bad.

Notes ⚠️

Start in DRY‑RUN, then backtest before live. This is not financial advice. Proper risk settings + sensible pair selection matter a lot.
